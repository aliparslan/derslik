The Preparatory Stage prepares youth for structured community involvement and leadership in service of the community.

---

## Objectives

1. Evaluate foundational mastery from Stages 1 & 2.
2. Understand the vital importance and obligation of community organization and unity.
3. Direct individual talents, bravery, and critical thinking toward constructive goals.

---

## Core Curriculum Focus

* **Quranic Memorization:** Last 3 Juz (Surah Al-Mujadilah to Surah An-Nas) and Surah Al-Kahf.
* **Hadith Studies:** *Riyad as-Salihin* (Gardens of the Righteous) — 40 chapters with memorization.
* **Creed (Aqidah):** The three aspects of Tawheed (Rububiyyah, Uluhiyyah, Asma wa Sifat) and core pillars of faith.
* **Fiqh & Usul:** Wisdoms of worship, history of legal schools (Madhabs), and introductory jurisprudence.
