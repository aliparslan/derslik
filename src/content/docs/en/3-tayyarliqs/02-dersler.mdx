## 1. Quranic Studies

* **Memorization:** Last 3 Juz of the Quran (Surah Al-Mujadilah to Surah An-Nas) and full Surah Al-Kahf.
* **Key Verse Analysis:** Ali 'Imran (31-32), Al-Baqarah (208), Saba (15-21), Al-A'raf (160-167), Ghafir (28-51), Al-Ma'idah (78-79).

---

## 2. Hadith Sciences

* Review Imam Nawawi's *40 Hadith*.
* Study 40 selected chapters from *Riyad as-Salihin* (Gardens of the Righteous) with memorization.

---

## 3. Islamic Creed (Aqidah)

* Rational proofs of the Creator, the three aspects of Tawheed (Rububiyyah, Uluhiyyah, Asma wa Sifat), and actions nullifying faith.

---

## 4. Jurisprudence (Fiqh & Usul)

* Wisdoms of worship, evolution of Islamic legal schools (Madhabs), and independent legal reasoning (Ijtihad).

---

## 5. Seerah & Companions

* **Key Events:** Siege of Banu Hashim, Isra and Mi'raj, Second Pledge of Aqabah.
* **Companions:** Mus'ab ibn 'Umair, Abdullah ibn Mas'ud, Khadijah (may Allah be pleased with them).
