This stage is designed for high school seniors, university students, and young adults aged 17 to 21.

---

## Core Objectives

1. **Firmness in Faith:** Strengthen faith and embody the Prophet’s sunnah in all aspects of life.
2. **Mosque Connection:** Maintain active involvement in the local mosque and community.
3. **Political & Social Awareness:** Develop sound understanding of global and local affairs affecting Muslims.
4. **Academic Excellence:** Excel in higher education, professional studies, and analytical skills.
5. **Balanced Lifestyle:** Engaging in halal recreation, athletics, and public community service.
