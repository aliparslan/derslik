## 1. Quranic Studies

* **Memorization:** Review Juz 30 and complete memorization of Juz 29 (Tabarak).
* **Target Verse Analysis:**
  - Surah Al-Ma'idah (Verses 3, 90-92)
  - Surah Al-Isra (Verses 28-38)
  - Surah Al-Mu'minun (Verses 1-11)
  - Surah An-Nur (Verses 1-3)
  - Surah Al-Furqan (Verses 63-67)
  - Surah Luqman (Verses 13-19)
  - Surah Al-Ahzab (Verses 1-6, 56-59)

---

## 2. Hadith Sciences

* Memorize and study commentary on Imam Nawawi's *Forty Hadith*.
* Principles of Hadith narration, authenticity, and classification.

---

## 3. Jurisprudence (Fiqh) & Ethics

* Detailed study of Salah, Sawm, Zakat, Hajj, clothing standards, photography/media rulings, family law, and non-Muslim relations.

---

## 4. History & Biographies

* **Seerah Events:** Ta'if campaign, Treaty of Hudaybiyyah, Conquest of Mecca.
* **Islamic Heroes:** Mu'adh ibn Jabal, Asma bint Abi Bakr, Nusaibah al-Maziniyyah, Tariq ibn Ziyad, Said ibn Jubayr.
* **Uyghur Figures:** Abdulqadir Damolla, Sultan Satuq Bughra Khan, Memtili Tewfiq, Abduxaliq Uyghur.
* **Historical Eras:** Caliphates, Qarakhanid Dynasty, Yarkent Khanate, modern East Turkistan history.
