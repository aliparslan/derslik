## 1. Quranic Studies & Recitation

* **Memorization:** Full 30th Juz of the Quran (Surah An-Naba to Surah An-Nas).
* **Quranic Narratives:**
  1. The Battle of the Trench (Khandaq)
  2. People of the Garden (Ashab al-Jannah)
  3. People of the Cave (Ashab al-Kahf)
  4. Story of Prophet Salih's She-Camel
  5. Story of Pharaoh (Fir'awn)
  6. Story of the Elephant (Ashab al-Fil)
* **In-Depth Tafsir:** Surahs Al-Fatiha, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Asr, Al-Qari'ah, and Al-Qadr.

---

## 2. Hadith & Daily Supplications (Duas)

* **Memorization:** 20 selected Hadiths with practical explanations.
* **Essential Supplications:**
  - Mealtime duas
  - Sleeping and waking up duas
  - Putting on new clothing
  - Entering & leaving the mosque
  - Boarding transportation
  - Istikhara (guidance) and travel supplications
* **Reference Books:** Imam Nawawi's *Forty Hadith*, *Hisn al-Muslim*.

---

## 3. Islamic Creed (Aqidah)

* Developing love for Allah and awareness of His constant presence.
* Understanding that true faith is inextricably linked with good actions.
* **Reference Books:** *Aqaid-i Zaruriyyah* (Abdulqadir Damolla), *Islamic Beliefs* (Muhammad Yusuf).

---

## 4. Practical Jurisprudence (Fiqh)

* Purification (Wudu/Taharah) rules and wisdom.
* Daily Salah (Prayer) rules, conditions, and etiquette.
* Fasting (Sawm) in Ramadan and voluntary fasts.

---

## 5. Biographies of the Prophets (Seerah)

1. **Prophet Adam (pbuh):** The creation of mankind and purpose.
2. **Prophet Noah (pbuh):** Perseverance in calling to truth.
3. **Prophet Abraham (pbuh):** Pure monotheism (Tawheed).
4. **Prophet Joseph (pbuh):** Truthfulness, patience, and integrity.
5. **Prophet Jesus (pbuh):** True identity and refuting false doctrines.

---

## 6. Uyghur Language & Literature

* Native language mastery, grammar, and official spelling guidelines.
* Reading historical Uyghur biographies and literary poetry.
