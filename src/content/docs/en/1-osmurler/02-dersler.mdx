## 1. Quranic Studies & Recitation

* **Memorization:** Full 30th Juz of the Quran (Surah An-Naba to Surah An-Nas).
* **Quranic Narratives:** (Reference Book: [*Stories of the Quran*](https://www.kalamullah.com/Books/Stories%20Of%20The%20Quran.pdf))
  1. [The Battle of the Trench (Khandaq)](http://qisse_link_1)
  2. [People of the Garden (Ashab al-Jannah)](http://qisse_link_2)
  3. [People of the Cave (Ashab al-Kahf)](http://qisse_link_3)
  4. [Story of Prophet Salih's She-Camel](http://qisse_link_4)
  5. [Story of Pharaoh (Fir'awn)](http://qisse_link_5)
  6. [Story of the Elephant (Ashab al-Fil)](http://qisse_link_6)
* **In-Depth Tafsir:** Surahs [Al-Fatiha](https://quran.com/1), [Al-Ikhlas](https://quran.com/112), [Al-Asr](https://quran.com/103), [Al-Qari'ah](https://quran.com/101), and [Al-Qadr](https://quran.com/97).

## 3. Islamic Creed (Aqidah)

* Developing love for Allah and awareness of His constant presence.
* Understanding that true faith is inextricably linked with good actions.
* **Reference Books:**
  - Abdulqadir Damolla: [*Aqaid-i Zaruriyyah*](https://www.Uyghurkitap.com/book/kitap-2212)
  - Muhammad Yusuf: [*Islamic Beliefs*](https://dinhayat.com/uploads/alqydt_alaslamyt_alakhyr.pdf)

---

## 4. Practical Jurisprudence (Fiqh)

* Purification (Wudu/Taharah) rules and wisdom.
* Daily Salah (Prayer) rules, conditions, and etiquette.
* Fasting (Sawm) in Ramadan and voluntary fasts.
* **Reference Books:** Muhammad Yusuf: *Religious Primer for Adolescents* ([Part 1](https://dinhayat.com/uploads/0000/osmir1.pdf) / [Part 2](https://dinhayat.com/uploads/0000/osmir2.pdf)), [*Rulings of Prayer*](https://dinhayat.com/uploads/re/ktab_alslat_lltb_alkhams.pdf).

---

## 5. Biographies of the Prophets (Seerah)

1. [**Prophet Adam (pbuh):**](https://archive.org/details/stories-of-the-prophets-by-ibn-kathir-english-pdf) The creation of mankind and purpose.
2. [**Prophet Noah (pbuh):**](https://archive.org/details/stories-of-the-prophets-by-ibn-kathir-english-pdf) Perseverance in calling to truth.
3. [**Prophet Abraham (pbuh):**](https://archive.org/details/stories-of-the-prophets-by-ibn-kathir-english-pdf) Pure monotheism (Tawheed).
4. [**Prophet Joseph (pbuh):**](https://archive.org/details/stories-of-the-prophets-by-ibn-kathir-english-pdf) Truthfulness, patience, and integrity.
5. [**Prophet Jesus (pbuh):**](https://archive.org/details/stories-of-the-prophets-by-ibn-kathir-english-pdf) True identity and refuting false doctrines.
* **Reference Book:** Ibn Kathir's [*Stories of the Prophets*](https://archive.org/details/stories-of-the-prophets-by-ibn-kathir-english-pdf).
