This initial stage targets middle school and high school students between the ages of 12 and 16. It establishes a foundational educational and spiritual framework.

---

## Stage Objectives

1. **Faith & Purity:** Nurture pure faith, upright character, modesty, and knowledge in young adolescents.
2. **Quranic Foundation:** Memorize Juz Amma (Juz 30), standard recitation (Tajweed), and understand core Quranic stories.
3. **Love for the Prophet:** Deepen love for Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) and righteous role models in Islamic history.
4. **Physical & Mental Wellbeing:** Guide youth safely through adolescent growth in a healthy, supportive environment.
5. **Talent Nurturing:** Identify and encourage individual talents, intelligence, and leadership qualities.
