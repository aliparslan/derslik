This stage focuses on candidates who have completed previous stages, training them for teamwork, organizational ethics, and collective leadership.

---

## Objectives

1. Deepen comprehensive scientific and practical understanding of Islamic jurisprudence.
2. Equip participants with organizational skills to serve community institutions.
3. Integrate individuals into structured teamwork and strategic planning initiatives.

---

## Key Modules

* **Quranic Studies:** Memorization & Tafsir of Surahs An-Nur, Yasin, Al-Hujurat, and Al-Mumtahanah.
* **Jurisprudence (Fiqh):** Commercial transactions, contracts, financial ethics, international relations in Islamic law.
* **Management & Organization:** Nature of structured collective work, administrative ethics, and institutional governance.
