## 1. Quranic Studies

* **Memorization:** Surahs An-Nur, Yasin, Al-Hujurat, and Al-Mumtahanah.
* **Key Verse Analysis:** Al-Baqarah (124-150, 159, 175-179, 195), An-Nisa (58-69), Al-Ma'idah (77-85), Yunus (60-66), Ibrahim (24-52), Al-Furqan (63-77).

## 3. Team Management & Strategy
