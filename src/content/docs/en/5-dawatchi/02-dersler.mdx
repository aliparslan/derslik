## 1. Advanced Quran & Hadith Studies

* **Memorization:** Surah Al-Ahqaf to Surah Al-Hadid; 40 Hadiths from *Al-Lu'lu' wal-Marjan*.
* **In-Depth Commentary:** Systematic thematic study of Al-Baqarah and Ali 'Imran.

---

## 2. Comparative Ideologies & World Religions

* Critical analysis of Christianity (Catholicism, Protestantism, Orthodoxy), Judaism, and Buddhism.
* Philosophical critique of Materialism, Secularism, and Darwinism.

---

## 3. Outreach & Dialogue Principles

* Interpersonal dialogue, cross-cultural communication, addressing misconceptions, and public discourse strategies.
