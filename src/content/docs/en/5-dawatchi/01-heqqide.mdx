This stage selects qualified candidates from previous stages to specialize in Dawah methodologies, comparative religious studies, public discourse, and social reform.

---

## Objectives

1. Equip specialists with effective communication methodologies and dialogue principles.
2. Develop broad awareness of global sociopolitical conditions and ideological trends.
3. Master advanced Quranic commentary, Hadith analysis, and jurisprudence.

---

## Curriculum Highlights

* **Advanced Tafsir:** Surah Al-Ahqaf to Surah Al-Hadid; comprehensive studies of Al-Baqarah & Ali 'Imran.
* **Comparative Religion:** Critical study of Christianity (Catholicism, Protestantism, Orthodoxy), Judaism, Buddhism, Materialism, and Secularism.
* **Social Jurisprudence:** Property law, contracts, insurance, endowments (Waqf), inheritance, and civil litigation.
