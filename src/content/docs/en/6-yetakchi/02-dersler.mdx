## 1. Advanced Quranic & Legal Theory

* **Memorization:** Surahs Yasin, At-Tawbah, and Al-Anfal.
* **Jurisprudential Theories:** Theory of Rights, Ownership, Social Security Guarantees, and Customary Law (Urf).

---

## 2. Reformist History & Leadership

* Biographies and strategic visions of Saladin, Al-Afghani, Hasan al-Banna, Omar Mukhtar, Abdulqadir Damolla, Sabit Damolla, Muhammad Emin Bughra, Isa Yusuf Alptekin, and Abdulhekim Mexsum.

---

## 3. Strategic Planning & Institutional Leadership

* Global Islamic institutions, international political analysis, executive mentorship, and long-term organizational strategy.
