The Master Mentor Stage trains educators, executive administrators, and institutional leaders capable of leading organizations, crafting strategic vision, and mentoring upcoming scholars.

---

## Objectives

1. Develop organizational management, governance, and executive decision-making skills.
2. Master strategic planning for long-term educational, cultural, and community institutions.
3. Train mentors capable of guiding the next generation of teachers and community leaders.

---

## Core Curriculum Focus

* **Advanced Tafsir & Quranic Sciences:** Detailed thematic commentary on Surahs Al-Ma'idah, Al-An'am, Al-Anfal, and At-Tawbah.
* **Jurisprudential Theory (Usul al-Fiqh):** Legal theory of rights, private ownership, social security guarantees, and customary law (Urf).
* **Biographies of Reformers:** Historical study of Saladin, Al-Afghani, Hasan al-Banna, Omar Mukhtar, Abdulqadir Damolla, Sabit Damolla, Muhammad Emin Bughra, Isa Yusuf Alptekin, and Abdulhekim Mexsum.
* **Institutional Governance & Strategy:** Global educational institutions, international law, diplomatic lobbying, crisis management, and strategic policy formulation.
