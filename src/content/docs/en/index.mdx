## Curriculum Stages

<CardGrid stagger>
	<StageCard title="1. Adolescents Stage (Ages 12 – 16)" href="/en/1-osmurler/01-heqqide/" icon="open-book">
		Foundations of faith (Aqidah), moral development, essential jurisprudence, Quran recitation, and native language literacy.
	</StageCard>
	<StageCard title="2. Youth Stage (Ages 17 – 21)" href="/en/2-yashlar/01-heqqide/" icon="notes">
		Intellectual & political awareness, media literacy, Uyghur literature, and practical worship skills.
	</StageCard>
	<StageCard title="3. Preparatory Stage" href="/en/3-tayyarliqs/01-heqqide/" icon="pencil">
		Preparation for community service, Usul al-Fiqh, historical analysis, and social organizational skills.
	</StageCard>
	<StageCard title="4. Organizational Stage" href="/en/4-tashkillash/01-heqqide/" icon="list-format">
		Collective service, management principles, strategic planning, and institution-building knowledge.
	</StageCard>
	<StageCard title="5. Dawah Specialist Stage" href="/en/5-dawatchi/01-heqqide/" icon="comment-alt">
		Methodologies of calling to faith, advanced Quran & Hadith studies, comparative religion, and social engagement.
	</StageCard>
	<StageCard title="6. Master Mentor Stage" href="/en/6-yetakchi/01-heqqide/" icon="star">
		Educational leadership, administrative excellence, curriculum design, strategic visionary planning, and mentorship.
	</StageCard>
</CardGrid>
