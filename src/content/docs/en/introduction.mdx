## Background & Vision

With the grace of Allah and the dedicated efforts of the scholars at the **East Turkistan Scholars Union**, this **Educational & Moral Training Program** was created specifically for Uyghur children and youth.

The spiritual, intellectual, and physical development of any community relies directly on the priority it places on structured education and learning. Without continuous education, awareness fades and ignorance takes root.

### Core Objectives

1. **Faith & Spiritual Foundation:** Cultivate a sound creed (Aqidah), upright morals, love for Allah and His Messenger, and proper practice of acts of worship.
2. **National Identity & Heritage:** Preserve and master the Uyghur language, literature, history, and cultural heritage.
3. **Capacity & Leadership:** Develop political awareness, media literacy, organizational acumen, and leadership skills to serve society effectively.

## The Six Educational Stages

The curriculum is structured logically into six progressive stages based on age and developmental maturity:

<Steps>

1. **1. Adolescents Stage (Ages 12 – 16):**
   A 4-year (or condensed 2-year) foundational curriculum covering faith, manners, essential jurisprudence, Quran recitation, and native language literacy.

2. **2. Youth Stage (Ages 17 – 21):**
   A 3-year curriculum focused on intellectual & political awareness, media literacy, Uyghur literature, and practical community engagement.

3. **3. Preparatory Stage:**
   A 2-year phase preparing students for active community service, social responsibility, and systematic Islamic jurisprudence (Usul al-Fiqh).

4. **4. Organizational Stage:**
   A 3-year phase centered on team management, organizational structure, strategic planning, and institution building.

5. **5. Dawah Specialist Stage:**
   Advanced specialization in community outreach (Dawah), comparative religion, Seerah research, and media communication.

6. **6. Master Mentor Stage:**
   Leadership mastery, executive management, curriculum design, strategic visionary planning, and mentoring future educators.

</Steps>
